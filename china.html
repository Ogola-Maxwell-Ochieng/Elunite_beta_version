Why China

How a handful of reform-era universities became a global study destination

China's push to internationalise its universities began in earnest in the early 2000s, when a growing number of institutions started teaching engineering, business, medicine and language programmes entirely in English to attract students who couldn't find comparable seats back home. Two decades on, hundreds of universities across the country enrol international students every year a scale most families researching study-abroad options don't expect until they start comparing course catalogues and scholarship numbers.

The arithmetic families keep landing on: recognised universities, English-medium teaching, and tuition a fraction of equivalent programmes in the West.

That scale is what makes China worth shortlisting, whatever field a student is chasing. English-medium instruction is standard at most partner universities, China Scholarship Council (CSC) awards fund full or partial tuition for many engineering, science and business seats, and medicine. Where We Place Students in China

China's recognised universities span major metros and mid-sized provincial cities alike, each with its own academic strengths and established international student communities. Here's where Pragati Consultancy has direct relationships with admission offices.

Beijing
Capital City

Highlights

China’s academic powerhouse, Beijing brings together elite universities, major research institutions, strong Mandarin-learning opportunities, and one of the country’s largest international student populations.

Shanghai
Global Hub

Highlights

Shanghai offers a highly international environment shaped by finance, commerce, design, and innovation, making it especially attractive for students who want global exposure alongside modern city living.

Guangzhou
Trade City

Highlights

Long connected to global trade and southern China’s business economy, Guangzhou combines respected universities, cultural diversity, and practical career links in logistics, commerce, and manufacturing.

Hangzhou
Innovation Hub

Highlights

Known for its digital economy and innovation ecosystem, Hangzhou appeals to students who want strong academic options in a polished, fast-evolving city with a more relaxed rhythm than the biggest metros.

Chengdu
Student City

Highlights

Chengdu stands out for its comfortable student lifestyle, comparatively lower living costs, growing university ecosystem, and a creative energy that makes it popular with both local and international students.

Xi’an
Historic Hub

Highlights

Xi’an blends deep historical character with a strong reputation in engineering, science, and research, giving students an affordable destination with serious academic depth and a distinctive cultural setting.
