Why Germany

The birthplace of the modern research university

Germany is one of the most respected study destinations in Europe and the model most universities around the world still follow, combining teaching and research under one roof. Since the founding of the University of Berlin in 1810, the country has remained strongly committed to public investment in higher education, which is one reason German universities continue to attract international students seeking globally recognised degrees, strong academic standards, and excellent research opportunities.

For families comparing options, it is rare elsewhere to find world-ranked engineering and research programmes with no tuition bill attached.

That makes Germany a strong shortlist choice across almost any field of study. Public universities are widely known for their academic reputation, international outlook, and structured admissions process, while many bachelor’s programmes are taught in German and a growing number of master’s programmes are available in English. Elunite helps students navigate accredited universities in Germany, match their academic profile to the right programme and language route, and support them through admission, the student visa process, blocked account preparation, and arrival in a new city.

Where We Place Students in Germany

German higher-education strength isn't concentrated in one city rather it's spread across regional hubs, each tied to a different industry and cost of living. Here's where Elunite has direct relationships with admission offices.

Bavaria
Engineering Hub

Highlights

Bavaria is one of the best places to study in Germany for international students, with Munich, TUM, strong engineering universities, and excellent career pathways in technology, mobility, and applied research.

Berlin
Startup Hub

Highlights

Berlin attracts international students with top universities, a wide choice of English taught master's programmes, and one of Europe’s most active startup and creative business ecosystems.

North Rhine-Westphalia
University Hub

Highlights

North Rhine-Westphalia is a top study destination in Germany because it offers one of the country’s largest concentrations of universities, with strong options in engineering, business, applied sciences, and research.

Baden-Württemberg
Automotive Hub

Highlights

Baden-Württemberg stands out for international students interested in automotive engineering, manufacturing, robotics, and applied innovation, with Stuttgart linked to major global industry names.

Saxony
Semiconductor Hub

Highlights

Saxony is a smart choice for students who want respected universities in Dresden and Leipzig, growing opportunities in semiconductors and microelectronics, and a more affordable student lifestyle.

Hesse
Finance Hub

Highlights

Hesse is best known for Frankfurt, one of Europe’s leading financial centres, making it especially attractive for international students in finance, economics, business, and management.

Public universities in almost every German state charge no tuition for bachelor's and consecutive master's programmes, for domestic and international students alike — you'll still pay a semester contribution of €150–€400, and Bavaria is the one state where a small number of non-EU programmes now carry tuition fees.

The blocked account (Sperrkonto) is a German bank account that proves you can cover a year's living expenses before your student visa is issued. The mandated amount for 2026 is €11,904, set by Germany's Federal Office for Migration and Refugees (BAMF).
