Our Destinations

🇮🇳 India
Expert

Access world-class universities, affordable tuition fees and globally recognised degrees in one of Asia's most sought-after destinations for international students pursuing STEM, MBA and medical programmes.

Key Cities:
Bangalore Mumbai New Delhi Hyderabad Gujarat Chandigarh
🇺🇸 USA
Expert

Home to Ivy League universities, thousands of scholarships and world-ranked programmes in business, engineering, medicine and the arts, the USA remains the most sought-after study abroad destination for ambitious international students.

Key Cities:
Boston New York San Francisco Los Angeles Chicago Houston
🇨🇳 China
Expert

From government-funded CSC scholarships and English-taught degree programmes to a low cost of living, China is fast becoming one of the world's most exciting study abroad destinations for medicine, engineering and business students.

Key Cities:
Beijing Shanghai Guangzhou Chengdu Nanjing Wuhan
🇩🇪 Germany
Expert

Tuition-free public universities, world-renowned engineering and research programmes and an 18-month post-study job-seeker permit make Germany one of the smartest and most rewarding study abroad destinations in Europe.

Key Cities:
Berlin Munich Frankfurt Hamburg Stuttgart Heidelberg
🇦🇺 Australia
Expert

Known for globally ranked universities, post-study work rights of up to 4 years and a clear pathway to permanent residency, Australia is one of the most rewarding study abroad destinations for international students pursuing engineering, nursing, IT and business programmes.

Key Cities:
Sydney Melbourne Brisbane Perth Adelaide Canberra
🇮🇪 Ireland
Expert

Boasting a 2-year post-study work permit, English-medium degrees and direct access to EU job markets, Ireland is a premier study abroad destination for international students looking to build global careers in technology and pharmaceuticals.

Key Cities:
Dublin Cork Galway Limerick Maynooth Waterford
