Why Australia

A leading study destination with global degrees and strong career pathways

Australia is one of the world’s most popular destinations for international students, known for globally recognised universities, high academic standards, multicultural cities, and strong graduate outcomes. Students choose Australia for its wide course selection, English-speaking learning environment, and balance of quality education with an active student lifestyle.

For many families, Australia offers the rare combination of respected universities, post-study opportunities, and a clear student support system.

From undergraduate degrees to specialised master’s programmes, Australia offers strong options across business, engineering, health, IT, hospitality, and research. Elunite helps students shortlist accredited Australian universities, prepare documents, understand tuition and living costs, arrange OSHC, and navigate the student visa subclass 500 process with clarity.

Where We Place Students in Australia

Australia’s higher-education strength is spread across major student cities, each with different tuition ranges, industries, lifestyle advantages, and post-study opportunities.

Sydney
Global City

Highlights

Sydney is one of the best places to study in Australia for international students, offering top universities, a strong job market, and broad exposure to finance, technology, media, and business.

Melbourne
Education Hub

Highlights

Melbourne is known for leading universities, student-friendly neighbourhoods, research excellence, and strong pathways in design, healthcare, engineering, and business.

Brisbane
Lifestyle Hub

Highlights

Brisbane attracts students with respected universities, a warmer climate, comparatively balanced living costs, and growing opportunities in business, science, and technology.

Perth
Mining Hub

Highlights

Perth is a strong choice for students interested in engineering, energy, geoscience, and applied industry pathways, with a calmer pace and good long-term study environment.

Adelaide
Affordable Hub

Highlights

Adelaide appeals to international students looking for quality universities, a manageable cost of living, and a supportive academic setting across health, engineering, and business.

Canberra
Research Hub

Highlights

Canberra is well suited to students seeking research strength, public policy, international relations, and a focused academic environment anchored by major universities.

Questions asked by students & parents

Most international students apply for the Student visa (subclass 500), which allows eligible students to study full-time in Australia and includes limited work rights during the course period.
Eligible student visa holders can work up to 48 hours per fortnight while their course is in session. Different rules can apply during breaks and for some research students.
OSHC stands for Overseas Student Health Cover. It is generally required for international students applying for an Australian student visa and helps cover basic medical and hospital care during study.
Yes. A Confirmation of Enrolment, often called a CoE, is a key document issued by your education provider before you lodge your student visa application.
The Genuine Student requirement applies to student visa applications and is used to assess whether study is the applicant’s primary purpose for coming to Australia.
