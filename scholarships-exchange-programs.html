The Struggle

Work From What's Actually Holding Your Funding Back

A scholarship or exchange file can stall because of a missed funding deadline, an eligibility mismatch, or an application that doesn't position your merit clearly enough. Advisory work can start with your academic record, financial need, and the scholarship or exchange programs you're targeting.

That profile-first approach keeps the advisory work concrete: identify what's blocking the funding, assess the related requirements, and make the next move from where the file actually stands. For broader guidance across study, work, and scholarship pathways, visit the full services page.

What We Handle Directly, and When We Refer Out

Scholarship and exchange advisory begins with your file. We can start directly with eligibility screening, funding search, application positioning, and exchange placement.

A

We Handle Directly

The full scholarship and exchange file, start to finish.

  • Fully funded and partial scholarship identification
  • International internship and exchange program matching
  • Application, essay, and interview positioning
  • Short-term mobility and research program placement
  • Professional training attachment coordination
  • Funding timeline and deadline tracking
B

When We Refer You Elsewhere

Outside our scope — we'll point you to the right partner.

  • Full university admissions applications
  • Employer-sponsored work visa cases
  • Standardized test coaching (IELTS, TOEFL, GRE, GMAT)
  • Legal or financial disputes with a scholarship provider

If a request falls outside scholarship and exchange advisory, we'll tell you directly and point you to the right specialist or partner.
