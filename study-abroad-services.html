The Struggle

Work From What's Actually Holding Your Application Back

A study abroad file can stall because of a mismatched university shortlist, a missed scholarship window, or a visa document that isn't in order. Advisory work can start with your transcripts and test scores, your budget and destination preferences, and the deadlines that are already on the clock.

That profile-first approach keeps the advisory work concrete: identify what's blocking the application, assess the related requirements, and make the next move from where the file actually stands. For broader guidance across study, work, and scholarship pathways, visit the full services page.

How Elunite Solves It

Profile & University Fit

Academic history, budget, and target countries are matched against realistic university and course options — not just the most-searched rankings.

Scholarship-First Applications

Statements of purpose, LORs, and transcripts are prepared alongside a scholarship search, so funding isn't an afterthought.

Visa & Documentation

Visa forms, financial proofs, and interview prep are reviewed against destination-specific requirements before filing.

Pre-Departure to Post-Arrival

Support doesn't end at your offer letter — we guide you through pre-departure checklists, travel planning, airport pickup, accommodation, banking, and local setup so you settle in confidently from day one.

What We Handle Directly, and When We Refer Out

Study abroad advisory begins with your file. We can start directly with profiling, shortlisting, applications, scholarships, and visa documentation.

A

We Handle Directly

The full study abroad file, start to finish.

  • Academic profiling and university & course shortlisting
  • Application, SOP, and LOR preparation
  • Scholarship and funding identification
  • Visa documentation review and mock interview preparation
  • Pre-departure briefings and post-arrival check-ins
B

When We Refer You Elsewhere

Outside our scope — we'll point you to the right partner.

  • Immigration or legal matters outside education visas
  • Standardized test coaching (IELTS, TOEFL, GRE, GMAT)
  • Credential evaluation handled by third-party agencies
  • Employer-sponsored work visa cases

If a request falls outside study abroad advisory, we'll tell you directly and point you to the right specialist or partner.
