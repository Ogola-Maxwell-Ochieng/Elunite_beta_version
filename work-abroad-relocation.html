The Struggle

Work From What's Actually Holding Your Placement Back

A work abroad file can stall because of a skills profile that doesn't match employer demand, documentation that isn't recruitment-ready, or a work permit route that isn't compliant with the destination country's rules. Advisory work can start with your work history, certifications, and the countries or industries you're targeting.

That profile-first approach keeps the advisory work concrete: identify what's blocking the placement, assess the related requirements, and make the next move from where the file actually stands. For broader guidance across study, work, and scholarship pathways, visit the full services page.
