How Our Programs Work

From your first consultation to the day you fly, here's exactly what happens when you enrol with Elunite.

01

Free Strategy Call

A free 30-minute consultation to assess your goals and match you with the right study abroad program.

02

Enrol & Profile Review

Your advisor reviews your academic profile and builds a personalised university admission strategy.

03

University Selection & Applications

We shortlist and apply to 50+ partner universities across India, UK, Canada, USA, and Germany.

04

Scholarship & Financial Aid

We identify and apply for scholarships, grants, and financial aid to fund your international education.

05

Visa Application & Approval

Our team handles your student visa application end-to-end, backed by a 95% visa success rate.

06

Pre-Departure & Arrival Support

We prepare you for life abroad, accommodation, culture, and everything you need before arrival.
