Why the USA

A university system built on choice, research funding and work rights

The United States is home to more than 4,000 accredited colleges and universities, and roughly 8,700 of them are certified by the Student and Exchange Visitor Program (SEVP) to enrol international students on an F-1 visa. That scale is what sets the US apart from almost any other study destination. A student choosing between a large public research university, a small private liberal arts college or a specialised technical institute often within the same state, with financial aid and merit scholarships attached to many of them.

For a family comparing options, the real draw isn't just the degree rather it's the year or more of legal work authorisation that comes with it.

That's the other half of the equation. Graduates on an F-1 visa can work in their field for up to 12 months through Optional Practical Training (OPT), and STEM graduates can extend that by a further 24 months nearly three years of US work experience before a student has to think about their next visa. Elunite exists to make this system navigable by verifying which universities are genuinely SEVP-certified, matching a student's academic profile and test scores to realistic options, and staying with them through the I-20, the DS-160, the visa interview, and their first weeks on campus.
